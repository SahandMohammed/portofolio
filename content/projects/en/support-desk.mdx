**Support Desk** is a business application focused on keeping customer support and internal service operations organized. It brings support requests, issue records, client information, and related administrative activities into a single working environment.

The project addresses a practical challenge: support work often spans conversations, documents, follow-ups, and several responsible people. Without a structured platform, progress and context can become difficult to track.

## Requirements & Operational Workflows

The application was shaped around real service-management needs: capturing support requests, retaining context, following up on issues, and making work visible to the appropriate users.

The broader workspace also includes client and branch records, bug tracking, documents, reporting, and quality-assurance workflows. The aim is to make these activities accessible through a common interface while keeping each workflow understandable.

## Technology & Application Development

The platform uses **Next.js, React, and TypeScript**, with **PostgreSQL** and **Prisma** supporting its data layer. The user interface uses modern component-based patterns, forms, and data-driven management views.

My work includes developing and maintaining operational screens and the application logic that connects them. The design prioritizes clear information organization, reusable interfaces, and workflows that support everyday service teams.

## Integrations & Media

Support records frequently involve attachments or supporting documentation. The application integrates with **Cloudflare R2** for file storage, keeping document handling separate from the main web application.

The project also includes a Telegram-based support intake integration, providing an additional channel for entering support requests. The public case study focuses on the integration's purpose rather than its internal processing, access configuration, or deployment design.
