The **Airport Safety Management System (SMS)** is a separate software project from the JTIA public website and administration platform. Its focus is the digital support of aviation safety-management activities within an operational environment.

Where a public airport website is designed to communicate information to travelers, a safety-oriented application must serve more specialized organizational work. That difference affects how requirements are interpreted and how users interact with the system.

## Domain & Requirements

Working on a safety-management application involves understanding the importance of structured information, clear user responsibilities, and workflows that are appropriate for an operational organization.

The project was approached as a dedicated application rather than an extension of the public airport website. Keeping the products conceptually separate helps reflect their different audiences and responsibilities.

## Software Delivery

The work includes application packaging for **Docker-based delivery**, providing a consistent format for distributing and operating the application.

This case study deliberately avoids listing unverified features, detailed safety procedures, or production performance claims. The project should be understood as a distinct aviation software engagement, with its technical scope described conservatively until more implementation details are ready for public presentation.

<Callout title="Domain-Focused Engineering">
  Different applications within the same organization can have fundamentally different users, requirements, and operational responsibilities. SMS was treated as its own software project rather than part of the airport's public-facing website.
</Callout>

## Project Perspective

The experience contributed to understanding how software requirements change across specialized domains. The key engineering theme is translating operational needs into structured digital experiences while preserving a clear boundary between public presentation and internal business software.
